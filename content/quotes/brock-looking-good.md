I was thinking about this quote in the gym this morning. As I watched people struggle to get that last rep in, I though "it's not pretty".
There are a couple of related quotes:

> People are jealous of what you've got. They're not jealous of how you got it.

-[Jimmy Carr](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/cwet4UNcn-0)

or [Alex Hormozi](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/mrSCwnmlZ8w)'s paraphrasing of it

> Everyone wants the view, no one wants the climb.

Back in [November](<{{ ref quotes/eisenhower-on-planning/ }}>) I referenced [The Effort is the Algorithm](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0xS68sl2D70). There is no success without struggle, failure, setback. It "looks bad". But it's the path to winning. [This (short) video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ILk8Yai3Wo) of Roger Federer is a slightly different take about each point being the most important thing until it's in the past when it's "just a point" carries some of the same themes.

All of which is to say, it's OK to struggle. It's necessary. No one doesn't struggle.

A dear family friend would often say, "Choose your hard". "Looking bad" is hard. But not growing is hard. Being beaten by someone who _is_ willing to look bad is hard too. You don't get to choose "easy", but you _do_ get to choose your hard.
